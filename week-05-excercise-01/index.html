Interactive Buttons

Six styles, six ways to respond. Hover over a button or use Tab to explore its transition.

Button Style 01: Color Fade

This button smoothly transitions its background and text colors on hover.

Button Style 02: Lift and Shadow

This button rises up and gains a shadow when you hover over it.

Button Style 03: Slide Fill

A purple fill slides across the button as its text turns white and its border deepens.

Button Style 04: Soft Zoom

This button gently grows while its background and border shift to a brighter blue.

Button Style 05: Border Glow

A soft teal halo appears as the border and text become more pronounced.

Button Style 06: Press Effect

This raised button moves down, shortens its shadow, and darkens to feel like a physical key.
